## Description

```text
Where’s my parcel?

Paste a tracking number, a carrier link or a whole shipping email, or scan the barcode on the label. Peek works out which of 3,500+ carriers has the parcel and shows its journey on a map, every scan and the day it should arrive.

No account needed. No ads.

WILL I KNOW WHEN IT MOVES?
Sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent to your email, and Peek notifies you: every scan, the important steps or delivery day only. An email can tell you it has arrived.

FOLLOWING MORE THAN ONE?
They share one list, the next to arrive on top, riding on its map. With an account, the same list is on your iPhone and on the web at peektracker.com. A widget keeps your next delivery on the Home Screen.

SOMEONE ELSE WAITING FOR IT?
Share the parcel’s page with a link. They need no app and no account. Wrap it as a gift, and what’s inside stays a surprise until it arrives.

A PASSPORT OF DELIVERIES
With an account, each delivery stamps your passport. Compare passports with friends; your parcels stay private.

3,500+ CARRIERS
Royal Mail, Evri, DPD, DHL, UPS, FedEx, GLS, An Post, USPS, Canada Post, Australia Post, La Poste, Swiss Post, PostNL, Correos, Poste Italiane, InPost, China Post, Cainiao, YunExpress and thousands more: national posts, couriers and marketplace shippers.

ALSO IN THE BOX
• A barcode scanner
• Sharing a number or a tracking link from Mail, Messages or Safari straight into Peek
• Light and dark themes
• English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Polish

PRIVATE AND OPEN SOURCE
Peek shows no ads and sells no data. It draws its own maps, so no map service sees where your parcels travel. A parcel followed without an account is forgotten 30 days after it arrives. Download your data or delete your account from the app, whenever you like. Every line of code is on GitHub: github.com/plhery/peek-delivery-tracker

Peek is independent of the carriers it reads. Their names belong to their owners.
```

## What's new

```text
• When a carrier doesn’t issue a tracking number, the parcel now says “Check the tracking number” and offers Change carrier, rather than saying it couldn’t get the latest update.
• When a parcel’s carrier needs the delivery postcode to find it, Peek now asks for it. If the carrier turns the postcode down, the parcel offers Update tracking details; if it finds no parcel with it, the parcel says so beside the postcode and offers Edit postcode.
• Change carrier shows the parcel’s current carrier at the top of the list.
• When the delivery scan has no place, the map shows the parcel delivered at its last stop if that stop is in the country it was going to, and otherwise where it was last seen.
• On a parcel’s card, the map’s place names no longer sit under the buttons at the top.
• A journey within a town or two shows as one place, and stops too close to tell apart share one mark.
• With your iPhone on its side, the globe keeps the route clear of the Dynamic Island.
• A scan written like “Haiger, DE” shows the country’s flag and name.
• Your note on “Did I get this one right?” can run to 4,000 characters, and shows how many are left near the end.
• On iPad, Change carrier and the Friends panels open in the middle of the screen, and Share opens beside its button.
```
